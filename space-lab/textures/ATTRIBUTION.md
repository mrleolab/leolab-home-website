# Planetary surface maps

Source and author: Solar System Scope / INOVE.

https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded 2K maps: Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day map, Mars,
Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Saturn ring alpha.

The original images are included unchanged. At runtime, the surface maps are
resized and assembled into a texture atlas, projected onto shaded spheres,
and rotated for the educational visualization. These are artistic planetary
maps based on NASA imagery; they are not live weather or observation images.

01 / THE BASICS

What is an
IB school?

An IB World School is a school authorized by the International Baccalaureate organization to offer one or more of its programmes. The approach emphasizes internationally minded learning, strong relationships, thoughtful questions and student agency.

IB schools may be public, independent or private. What matters is that the school has completed the IB authorization process for the programme it offers.

02 / PRIMARY YEARS PROGRAMME

For young
inquirers.

The IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) is designed for children ages 3–12. It is an inquiry-based, transdisciplinary framework: students investigate meaningful questions and connect ideas across subjects.

Rather than only memorizing facts, learners build conceptual understanding, practice collaboration and reflection, and take increasing ownership of their learning.

Newton’s gravity

aᵢ = G Σⱼ≠ᵢ mⱼ (rⱼ − rᵢ) / |rⱼ − rᵢ|³

Every body attracts every other body. Velocity Verlet advances position and velocity with a fixed integration step; simulation rate controls how many days pass per second.

Kepler & orbital energy

T = 2π √(a³ / μ) · μ = G(M + m)

ε = v²/2 − μ/r and a = −μ/(2ε). The displayed period exists only for bound orbits. Changing mass or G preserves current velocity, then changes acceleration.

What the model represents

Distances use AU; velocities use km/s. Body sizes are enlarged for visibility. Initial elements are J2000 approximations (Earth represents the Earth–Moon barycenter). This educational model omits relativity and collision dynamics. Large energy drift signals that a smaller step is needed.

01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE LAB

Curiosity first.
Keep building.

I’m Leo, and Leo Lan’s Science Community is my personal space for exploring computer science, science, and AI. I created this lab to connect what I’m learning with what I can build—and to make useful discoveries easier to share.

I’m developing my skills one project, question, and challenge at a time. That means learning the foundations, trying new tools, experimenting with code, and returning to the ideas I don’t understand yet.

This website is a record of that journey, not a finished textbook. It brings together projects, university information, learning resources, and experiments that help me think more deeply about technology.

03 / HOW I WANT TO LEARN

Learn openly.
Share generously.

My goal is to build a meaningful path in computer science while growing my science and AI skills. I want to understand the ideas beneath the tools, not just follow the latest trend.

I use this space to organize resources, test ideas, and document what I discover. I value clear explanations, reliable sources, and the willingness to ask a basic question. Learning is more useful when someone else can build on it, too.

Explore database administration, document databases, and modern data-platform engineering. These are related but distinct specializations.

## Choose a path

Oracle’s certification catalog covers several technologies; choose the database path relevant to you. MongoDB has its own certification resources. Databricks focuses on its data and AI platform rather than general-purpose database administration.

## Official certification resources

- [Oracle Certifications](https://education.oracle.com/oracle-certification-path)
- [MongoDB Certifications](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/certification)
- [Databricks Certifications](https://www.databricks.com/learn/training/certification)

## A practical first step

Practice schema or document design, queries, access control, and backup/restore. Pick one platform first and use non-sensitive sample data.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.
:::

Develop defensive security knowledge, risk awareness, and practical skills for protecting systems and networks.

## Choose a path

ISC2 offers a range of credentials; Fortinet and Palo Alto Networks publish product-focused certification portfolios. Vendor-specific and broader professional credentials serve different purposes.

## Official certification resources

- [ISC2 Certifications](https://www.isc2.org/certifications)
- [Fortinet Certifications](https://www.fortinet.com/training-certification)
- [Palo Alto Networks Certifications](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/services/education/certification)

## A practical first step

Start with fundamentals if you are new. Check experience and endorsement requirements before choosing an advanced credential. Run exercises only in systems you own or have explicit permission to test.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.
:::

Technical work also needs planning, delivery, communication, and risk management.

## Choose a path

PMI’s official catalog includes entry and advanced project-management options. Check the current eligibility rules, education requirements, and experience requirements before choosing a route.

## Official certification resources

- [PMI Certifications](https://www.pmi.org/certifications)

## A practical first step

Plan a small project with scope, milestones, risks, and a short retrospective. A professional certification is not a replacement for hands-on delivery experience.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.
:::

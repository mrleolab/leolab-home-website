Understand AI concepts, responsible use, and how AI services fit into applications. Choose a fundamentals credential if you are beginning; use provider catalogs to explore engineering and infrastructure paths later.

## Choose a path

Compare Microsoft’s Azure AI Fundamentals and AWS Certified AI Practitioner with your target platform. NVIDIA’s catalog offers additional AI and infrastructure options. These are different credentials, not interchangeable exams.

## Professional certifications

- [Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-fundamentals/)
- [AWS Certified AI Practitioner](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-practitioner/)
- [NVIDIA Certifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/)
- [PyTorch Certified Associate (PTCA)](https://pytorch.org/pytorch-certification/) — a certification from Linux Foundation Education and the PyTorch Foundation for early-stage practitioners.

Explore model development, agents, and practical AI use alongside cloud credentials. The credential type matters: a course certificate or completion badge is not the same as a professional certification exam.

## AI infrastructure: vLLM & SGLang

These are technical learning paths, not verified certification programs. No official individual certification was confirmed in the project resources reviewed on **October 26, 2026**. Third-party course certificates should not be described as certifications issued by these projects.

- [vLLM documentation](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/) — study model serving, batching, memory use, and performance benchmarking.
- [SGLang documentation](https://docs.sglang.io/) — study LLM and multimodal serving, prefix caching, and distributed inference.

For a portfolio exercise, serve the same small model with each framework and record hardware, model version, latency, throughput, and memory use. Consult hardware requirements first: inference experiments may need a GPU or paid cloud compute. Do not expose an unauthenticated model server to the internet.

## Suggested learning sequence

1. Start with AI fundamentals and an introductory Claude or OpenAI learning resource.
2. Complete a Hugging Face course project and check its certificate requirements.
3. Build and explain a small PyTorch model; consider PTCA if a formal credential fits your goals.
4. Explore vLLM or SGLang when you are ready for inference infrastructure.

## A practical first step

Build a small experiment, explain its limitations, and document what data it uses. Then read the official skills outline and identify concepts you cannot yet explain.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.

Understand AI concepts, responsible use, and how AI services fit into applications. Choose a fundamentals credential if you are beginning; use provider catalogs to explore engineering and infrastructure paths later.

## Choose a path

Compare Microsoft’s Azure AI Fundamentals and AWS Certified AI Practitioner with your target platform. NVIDIA’s catalog offers additional AI and infrastructure options. These are different credentials, not interchangeable exams.

## Official certification resources

- [Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-fundamentals/)
- [AWS Certified AI Practitioner](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-practitioner/)
- [NVIDIA Certifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/)

## A practical first step

Build a small experiment, explain its limitations, and document what data it uses. Then read the official skills outline and identify concepts you cannot yet explain.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.
:::

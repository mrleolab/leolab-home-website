Build your understanding of addressing, routing, switching, troubleshooting, and network operations.

## Choose a path

Cisco and Juniper provide official certification pathways. Compare the current prerequisites and skills outline with the equipment or virtual lab you can access.

## Official certification resources

- [Cisco Certifications](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/training-certifications/certifications/index.html)
- [Juniper Certifications](https://www.juniper.net/us/en/training/certification.html)

## A practical first step

Practice subnetting, inspect a routing table, and troubleshoot a simple connectivity failure. Use a legal simulator or your own equipment; certification study is not permission to access other networks.

:::note Check before registering
Names, exam versions, fees, eligibility, and renewal policies can change. Follow the provider’s current documentation. A course completion certificate or badge is not necessarily a professional certification.
:::

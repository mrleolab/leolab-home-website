Explore every competition entry previously listed in Leo Lab's menu: nine official entry points across mathematics and physics in the USA and Canada. This is a guide to those entries, not an exhaustive directory of every competition worldwide.

**Official sources reviewed: October 26, 2026.** Dates below identify the season. A past exam date is not an upcoming registration opportunity. This guide is not automatically updated.

## Choose a subject and region

- [Mathematics · USA](./math-usa.md): MAA AMC and MATHCOUNTS.
- [Mathematics · Canada](./math-canada.md): Waterloo CEMC, Euclid, and COMC.
- [Physics · USA](./physics-usa.md): F=ma / USAPhO and PhysicsBowl.
- [Physics · Canada](./physics-canada.md): CAP Prize Exam and Sir Isaac Newton Exam.

## Before registering

1. Check grade, age, location and nationality/residency rules; these can differ between entry rounds and national-team selection.
2. Ask your school about hosting and its internal deadline, which may be earlier than the organizer's deadline.
3. Confirm the current year, time zone, fees, permitted equipment and supervision rules on the official pages linked in each guide.
4. Use organizer-provided past papers to choose an appropriate level. Never assume that buying a preparation course registers you for the competition.

:::note For Canadian families
An event organized in the USA is not necessarily open to a student sitting in Canada. In particular, read the F=ma and USAPhO location and eligibility restrictions before making plans.
:::

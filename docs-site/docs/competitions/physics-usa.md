## AAPT — F=ma & USAPhO {#fma}

A high-school physics selection pathway, not two interchangeable open-entry exams. Qualification leads from F=ma to USAPhO.

- **Eligibility:** F=ma requires U.S. citizenship, permanent residence, or attendance at a U.S. school, and the student must sit the exam in the USA. USAPhO requires U.S. citizenship or permanent residence and a U.S. exam location.
- **Register:** arrange an eligible proctor through the official registration process. Parents/relatives cannot proctor; paid test-preparation centres are not accepted proctors.
- **2026 reference only:** F=ma February 12; USAPhO April 10. Next-season dates were not confirmed in the reviewed schedule.
- **Official links:** [program](https://www.aapt.org/physicsteam/PT-landing.cfm) · [rules and eligibility](https://www.aapt.org/physicsteam/PT-rules.cfm) · [2026 schedule](https://www.aapt.org/physicsteam/2026/ImportantDates.cfm).

## AAPT PhysicsBowl {#physicsbowl}

International high-school physics: 40 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes under school supervision. Division I is for first-year physics students; Division II is for second-year students.

- **When:** the 2026 window was March 18–April 3. The official page says 2027 registration opens in December; do not reuse the 2026 deadline.
- **Register:** a teacher/school follows the organizer's registration instructions; some countries use designated regional organizers.
- **Official source:** [PhysicsBowl overview, registration and exam information](https://www.aapt.org/Programs/PhysicsBowl/).

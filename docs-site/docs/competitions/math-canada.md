## Waterloo CEMC Contests {#cemc}

A family of mathematics and computing contests for grades 5–12, not one exam. Examples include Pascal/Cayley/Fermat (grades 9–11) and senior/intermediate mathematics contests.

- **2026–27 examples:** senior/intermediate mathematics: November 18, 2026 in the Americas; Pascal/Cayley/Fermat: February 23–24, 2027 in the Americas. Other contests and overseas dates vary.
- **Register:** through a school, not an individual student account. New school-account approval can take up to three weeks. Order deadlines depend on the contest and country.
- **Official links:** [full calendar and grade filters](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests) · [school registration process](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/how-contests-work).

## Euclid Mathematics Contest {#euclid}

Senior-secondary mathematics combining short answers with written solutions. Intended for final-year secondary or CÉGEP students; motivated younger students may participate.

- **2027 dates:** April 6 in the Americas; April 7 elsewhere. Ordering deadline: March 11, 2027.
- **Register:** ask your mathematics teacher to arrange a supervised school entry through CEMC.
- **Official links:** [Euclid requirements and dates](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/euclid) · [past papers and solutions](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/resources/past-contests).

## Canadian Open Mathematics Challenge — COMC {#comc}

A 2.5-hour high-school mathematics challenge with pathways to further Canadian mathematical competitions. Participation and eligibility for awards or team selection are separate questions.

- **2026 dates:** October 29 in the Americas; October 30 elsewhere. Registration was closed when reviewed; this is not a 2027 announcement.
- **Register:** follow the CMS organizer portal or its independent-student/open-writing-centre instructions. Confirm current eligibility and local availability.
- **Official source:** [COMC overview, registration status, eligibility and FAQs](https://cms.math.ca/competitions/comc/). The organizer announced administration changes for 2026; do not rely on an older preparation provider's instructions.

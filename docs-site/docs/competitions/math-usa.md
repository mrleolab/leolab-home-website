## MAA AMC — AMC 8, 10 & 12 {#amc}

Mathematical problem-solving contests at different school levels. AMC 8 targets grade 8 and below; AMC 10 and 12 target grade 10 and 12 and below respectively. Check age limits and other conditions in the [official policies](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/maa-american-mathematics-competitions-policies/).

- **When:** 2026 AMC 10/12 A: November 5; B: November 13. 2027 AMC 8: January 21–27.
- **Register:** ask a participating school or competition manager. Teachers register the host institution; students should confirm a seat with their host.
- **Deadlines:** new managers' regular deadline for 2026 AMC 10/12 was October 15; AMC 8 regular registration ends January 5, 2027. Returning-manager and additional-bundle deadlines differ.
- **Official links:** [AMC overview](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/) · [dates and registration](https://maa.org/amcreg/).

## MATHCOUNTS {#mathcounts}

Live individual and team mathematics for grades 6–8. Eligibility depends on school participation and program rules; non-school competitor entry is conditional.

- **2026–27 season:** school rounds November–January; chapter rounds February; state rounds March; national competition May 9–10, 2027.
- **Register:** start with your school coach. If your school does not participate, read the non-school competitor rules before a parent registers; that route opens November 5, 2026.
- **Official links:** [program and registration](https://www.mathcounts.org/programs/mathcounts-competition-series) · [rules, eligibility and calendar](https://www.mathcounts.org/programs/competition-rules-faq).

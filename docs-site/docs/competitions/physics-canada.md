## CAP High School / CÉGEP Prize Exam {#cap}

A challenging senior-school physics exam, with multiple-choice and written problems.

- **Register:** ask your physics teacher to contact the provincial coordinator; local ordering deadlines and award eligibility need confirmation.
- **Timing:** the Saskatchewan coordinator's official 2026 announcement lists April 8, with a local request deadline of March 27. These dates have passed and are not 2027 dates. A 2027 national schedule was not confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Official sources:** [CAP student awards and exam entry](https://cap.ca/programs/recognitions/high-school-students/cegep-prize-exam/) · [2026 coordinator announcement](https://research-groups.usask.ca/physics-high-school-outreach/documents/full-announcement-2026.pdf).

## Waterloo Sir Isaac Newton Exam {#sin}

High-school physics problem solving, open to any grade, including participants outside Canada.

- **Register:** a teacher registers students and supervises the online exam; self-administered tests are not permitted.
- **When:** usually early May, but the official page specifically lists **April 29, 2027** for the 2027 contest. Registration information says March; confirm the current opening date and status with the organizer because older notices remain on the page.
- **Official source:** [SIN format, registration and current contest date](https://uwaterloo.ca/centre-advanced-science-education/science-contests/sir-isaac-newton-exam).
